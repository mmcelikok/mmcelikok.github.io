A central theme of my work is how autonomous agents learn to act well **with** other agents, rather than in isolation: cooperating with populations of socially rational agents {% cite bandyopadhyay2025complexity %}, learning uncoupled Stackelberg equilibria under commitments {% cite loftin2024uncoupled %}, scaling multi-agent RL to large networked systems via distributed influence-augmented simulators {% cite suau2022distributed %}, and reasoning about multi-stage planning with foundation policies through hitting-time isomorphisms {% cite boock2026hitting %}.
