Effective AI assistants need models of the humans and agents they work with. This line of work develops Bayes-adaptive POMDP formulations for best-response planning with human "centaur" partners {% cite celikok2022bestresponse %}, abstraction techniques for tractable Bayesian RL in factored POMDPs {% cite starre2025abstraction %}, differentiable models of user behaviour {% cite hamalainen2023differentiable %}, machine teaching of active sequential learners {% cite peltola2019machine %}, and interactive AI systems built around an explicit theory of mind {% cite celikok2019interactive %}.
