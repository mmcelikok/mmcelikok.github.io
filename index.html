Mustafa Mert Çelikok

Assistant Professor in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, University of Southern Denmark

Department of Mathematics and Computer Science

University of Southern Denmark

Odense, Denmark

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the University of Southern Denmark, in the Data Science Section of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science (IMADA). My research is in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), with a focus on how autonomous agents learn to cooperate, compete, and act as effective assistants for people and for other agents — drawing on game theory, Bayesian and model-based RL, and models of other agents’ beliefs and intentions (“theory of mind”).

Before joining SDU, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Delft University of Technology, working on multi-agent reinforcement learning for collaborative AI systems as part of the Dutch Hybrid Intelligence Centre. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Aalto University, advised by Professor Samuel Kaski, on model-based multi-agent reinforcement learning for AI assistants, and I was a member of the first cohort of ELLIS PhD students.

I am always happy to hear from prospective students and collaborators interested in multi-agent learning, cooperative AI, and human-AI interaction — see the CV page for my full background and the publications page for my papers.
